# Lithica Atlas

**Geoscience reference at hand.** Explore minerals, rocks, textures, fossils, structures and other geoscience collections through visual records and bibliographic references.

## What you can do

- Search and filters across collections and diagnostic properties.
- Images, citations, tables, glossary and reference guides.
- Six interface languages, with fallback when a record has no translation.
- A catalog installed in modules, available locally during a verified session.

## Use and availability

Initial setup requires connectivity, free storage and time to download the catalog. Sign-in and account renewal require online verification. Translation availability varies by record; references support professional judgment.

**Status:** Available on Google Play.
