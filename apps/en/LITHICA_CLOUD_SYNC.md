# Lithica Cloud Sync

**From field backups to QGIS.** Find and open Explorer and Mapper project backups stored in your Google Drive directly in QGIS.

## What you can do

- Google account selection through desktop OAuth authorization.
- A grouped backup inventory and local search after refreshing the list.
- Download and validation of the selected project.
- GeoPackage layer and table loading for desktop review.

## Use and availability

Requires QGIS 3.34 or later. Drive access is read-only: changes made in QGIS are not automatically sent back to Explorer or Mapper. Keep independent backups of important campaigns.

**Status:** QGIS plugin · v2.0.4.
