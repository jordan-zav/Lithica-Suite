# Lithica Mapper

**Geological mapping with a stylus.** Draw units, contacts, faults and structures in a mapping workspace designed for Android tablets, combining stylus editing and touch navigation.

## What you can do

- Freehand, vertex and mixed drawing, with selection, transformation and undo.
- Editable layers, styles, structural symbols and geological patterns.
- GeoPackage, vector import, and image and PDF georeferencing.
- Prepared offline maps, terrain, stratigraphic columns and GIS export.

## Use and availability

Check units, datum and coordinate reference systems when combining data. Account verification needs connectivity; prepared local work can continue offline. Drive is optional and its backups can be read in QGIS through Cloud Sync.

**Status:** Available on Google Play.
