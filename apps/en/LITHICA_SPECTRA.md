# Lithica Spectra

**From satellite scenes to interpretation.** Organize study areas and scenes for acquisition, multiband visualization and local desktop spectral processing.

## What you can do

- Image and elevation acquisition through user-configured providers.
- RGB compositions, band algebra and terrain products.
- Workflows for ASTER, Sentinel-2 L2A and Landsat 8/9 Level-2.
- Spectral Angle Mapper and spectral-analysis references.

## Use and availability

Band availability depends on the product and each file's metadata. Review coefficients and inputs before running a recipe. Resampling does not add spatial detail. No verified public installer is available.

**Status:** In development.
