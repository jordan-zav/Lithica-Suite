# Lithica Explorer

**Your geological field notebook.** Record observations with their spatial context: location, photographs, geometry and descriptions within a field project.

## What you can do

- Georeferenced observations with media evidence and associated documents.
- Maps, GIS layers and areas prepared for offline fieldwork.
- Structural tools, rock classification and supporting diagrams.
- GIS export and optional project backups in Google Drive.

## Use and availability

Prepare areas and files before leaving coverage. First sign-in, account renewal, online sources and Drive need connectivity. Importing a display layer does not mean all of its geometries become editable.
