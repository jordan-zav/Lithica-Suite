# Lithica GeoTech Community

**Geotechnical reference in an open edition.** An independent community edition for material reference and geotechnical calculations in Flutter.

## What you can do

- RMR and Q-System classifications.
- Hoek-Brown criterion and ISRM reference material.
- A geotechnical material reference library.
- Source code available under the repository's GPL license.

## Use and availability

Community and GeoTech are maintained separately. Features, tests or publications of the proprietary edition do not establish the same availability in Community. Review license and attribution requirements when redistributing.

**Status:** Community edition · GPL.
