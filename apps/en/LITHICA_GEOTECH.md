# Lithica GeoTech

**Geotechnical reference and assessment.** Bring material references, rock-mass classifications and strength criteria into a technical workspace for field and office work.

## What you can do

- RMR, Q-System and support charts.
- Hoek-Brown and equivalent Mohr-Coulomb parameters.
- ISRM suggested methods and reference properties with context.
- Engineer and Expert modes, with local project and structure records.

## Use and availability

Results depend on inputs, units and the method version. Reference properties do not replace project measurements. Closed testing requires an eligible account; Community is a separate edition.
