## A family of geoscience tools

Lithica brings together applications and resources for field geology, mapping, geotechnics, scientific reading, modeling and remote sensing. Each tool supports part of the workflow: recording evidence in context, building maps, consulting references or exploring an interpretation.

Local work and offline preparation are central to the field applications. Account features, data acquisition and synchronization have their own connectivity requirements.

## From fieldwork to the desktop

1. **Prepare** the area, layers and documents you will need beyond network coverage.
2. **Record** observations and evidence in Explorer or **edit** geological maps in Mapper.
3. **Consult** Atlas references and GeoTech tools; **annotate and study** in Notebook.
4. **Export** GIS files or use optional Drive backups to open projects in QGIS through Cloud Sync.

Applications keep their own projects. Cloud Sync is a read-only bridge to QGIS: edits made there are not automatically returned to the apps. Keep independent copies of important campaigns.

## Downloads and availability

**Explorer, Mapper and Atlas** are available on Google Play for Android. **GeoTech** offers access through closed testing. **Cloud Sync** is distributed as a QGIS plugin. Notebook, GeoModeller and Spectra remain in development; Academy is educational material.

See **[Downloads and access](RELEASES.en.md)** for the appropriate channel. Platform support in a project does not establish that a public installer is already available.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to install the entire Suite?**

Choose the tool that fits your task. There is no single installer for the whole family.

**Can I work offline?**

The field applications support work with locally prepared resources. First sign-in, account renewal, online sources and Drive need connectivity. Check access and files before leaving coverage.

**Is the entire Suite open source?**

Each product has its own terms. Cloud Sync and GeoTech Community have public repositories and licenses listed on their product pages. This public catalog does not grant a common license over the other applications' code, branding or content.

**Where can I get help or send feedback?**

Visit the [GisGeo Dev website](https://gisgeo.dev/portfolio/lithica-suite/) and [contact page](https://gisgeo.dev/contact/). Cloud Sync and Community also accept issues in their repositories. Avoid posting personal data or confidential project information.
